CONCLUSION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The Riemann Hypothesis is not yet formally proven by this framework. The
framework is not an analogy — it is a geometric-spectral structure with
algebraically established components and a precisely identified gap.

Established:
  1. The symmetry axis Re(s)=1/2 is the algebraic fixed set of J_s (2 lines)
  2. The same axis is the radial fixed boundary of J_N under the coordinate map
  3. J_N is an algebraically exact four-cycle with Re(s)=1/2 = (π/2)/π
  4. φ is the algebraically verified attractor of (J_N ∘ recursion)
  5. H/4 = ħ_NN·(π/2) is the step SIZE at the φ-crossing (not step count)
  5a. H/4 is DERIVED from J_N geometry: the J_N quarter-turn (π/2 per step)
     applied to the SMIP action quantum (H_NN = ħ_NN·2π) over one orbit step
     gives H_NN/4 = ħ_NN·(π/2). H_NN corresponds to h (Planck constant, full
     gauge orbit action); ħ_NN = H_NN/2π corresponds to ħ (reduced, per radian).
     The π/2 factor is J_N's angular generator — the same factor that produces
     Re(s) = (π/2)/π = 1/2 in Corollary 2.5 (Theorem 2.14)
  6. Formal precedents (Selberg, Deligne, Wiles) establish that in precisely
     analogous settings, zeros are forced to the critical boundary by
     spectral / cohomological mechanisms
  7. The SMIP Lagrangian provides a concrete Hilbert-Pólya candidate with
     Noether-conserved currents verified at 7+σ
  8. The entropy/inertia crossing theorem establishes T* = Ω·T_Planck
     algebraically (machine epsilon verification), independently grounding
     d* = 0.24600 as the tangency coordinate of the information-theoretic
     ceiling and floor — a third independent derivation of Re(s)=1/2

  Prove that ζ(s) under J_N action on S² transforms as the l=1, m=0
  spherical harmonic Y_1^0. Given this, the Courant Nodal Domain Theorem

The SMIP Hamiltonian is the leading candidate for (a)+(b). Point (c) is
