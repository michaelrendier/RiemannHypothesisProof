Aizawa attractor vs the ζ pathway in Universal Native Space

webgl_raw WebGL 1.0canvas2d panels

Two 3-D views under one mouse-driven camera: the Aizawa attractor integrated live (RK4), and ζ(½ + it) for the first 25,000 zeros in three charts (Riemann sphere, UNS cylinder = the spring, UNS flat). Prime resonance ticks, the equator ring |ζ| = 1, the zeros on the axis, the prime spring (Euler path) at a chosen height, and the zero spectrum with spikes at ln pk. The “grow” button draws the path zero by zero.

URL options: ?chart=sphere|cyl|flat &view=pole|eq &zeros=N &ticks=0|2|3|5|7|all. Data: zeta_uns_path_data.js, built by experiments/build_zeta_uns_viz_data.py from the engine ValaQuenta/modules/zeta_uns_path.
