The ζ pathway growing, beside the Aizawa attractor

webgl_raw WebGL 1.0canvas2d panels

An animation of ζ(½ + it) through all 25,000 zeros we have: 100 zeros per step, 250 steps, one step per second (250 s), with the Aizawa attractor (integrated live, RK4) growing beside it under one mouse-driven camera. The prime spring (the chain of prime steps at the current height) and the zero spectrum (spikes at ln pk) update with every step, and a readout gives the mean value of ζ so far and the share of the path in Re ζ < 0, the sparse side seen from the pole. Three charts: Riemann sphere, UNS cylinder (the spring), UNS flat. Sources and literature are cited on the page.

URL options: ?chart=sphere|cyl|flat &view=pole|eq|tilt &step=1..250 &play=0 &ticks=0|2|3|5|7|all. Data: zeta_uns_path_data.js, built by experiments/build_zeta_uns_viz_data.py from ValaQuenta/modules/zeta_uns_path. Code: michaelrendier/RiemannHypothesisProof.
