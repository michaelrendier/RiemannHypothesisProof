Aizawa: dφ/dt = d (constant), dr/dt = (z − b) r, and r can never reach the axis. ζ on the critical line: ζ = e−iθ(t)·Z(t), angle −θ(t) turning at −θ′(t) = −½ ln(t/2π), radius the signed Z(t); Z′/Z is the sum over the zeros of the pole kernels 1/(t − γ), and the zeros are exactly the axis passages Aizawa forbids. In the cylinder chart the zeros sit on the axis at height γ. In the sphere chart every zero is the south pole and the equator ring is |ζ| = 1.
