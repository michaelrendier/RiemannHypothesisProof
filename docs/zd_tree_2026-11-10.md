## Definition (assessors, De Marrais / Moreno)
For imaginary slots a < b, δ = a ⊕ b: e_a + e_b is a zero divisor iff some e_c ± e_{c⊕δ} annihilates it. The 2^k − 1 XOR differences δ are the nodes of the tree.

- **The 15 differences of T_16 split 7 + 8:** the seven ZD loci are δ = 9 … 15 (one box-kite of 6 assessors each, one per Fano line of 𝕆), and the eight clean δ are 1 … 8.
- **Descent rule** (δ → {δ, δ + 2^k}): at each doubling every old class is kept (a ZD-carrying class stays carrying, a clean class stays clean), and of the 2^k new differences all but the pure power 2^k carry zero divisors (15, 31, 63, 127 new carrying classes; the clean new class is 16, 32, 64, 128). **Clean corridors = {1, …, 7} ∪ {2^j : 3 ≤ j ≤ k − 1}: the octonion differences and the pure doublings, k + 4 of them.** Carrying classes = 2^k − k − 5 exactly (7, 22, 53, 116, 243; 498 at T_512 in the source's table).
- **A zero divisor, once born, is immortal:** no pair's status ever changes at a later doubling (0 of the old pairs changed at every level); the new zero divisors are all pairs that involve an index in the new upper half.
- **Survivors halve, the zero divisors take over:** survivors 63, 171, 435, 1,059, 2,499 obey s(k+1) = 2 s(k) + 3(2^k − 1) at every level; their share of the pairs falls by 0.61, 0.59, 0.58 per doubling toward ½; the ZD share tends to 1. Among the *new* pairs at each level the survivors are 30.0 %, 17.7 %, 10.3 %, 5.9 %: the same halving.
- **Box-kite components** (from `zd_propagation.py`, run by me): T_16 seven components of 6; T_32 22 components of sizes {12, 14}; T_64 53 of {24, 28, 30}; T_128 116 of {48, 56, 60, 62}: sizes 2^(k−1) − 2^j; the seven root box-kites double in size each level (6 → 12 → 24 → 48).
- **The zero-divisor incidence equals the anticommutation graph** used in the slot-rule test, exactly as sets for T_16 … T_128: the "deforming pairs" there are the zero-divisor tree's incidence matrix.
